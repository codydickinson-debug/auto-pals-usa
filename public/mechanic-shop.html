What We Do

Service, done right

We'll take on nearly any repair, but our bread and butter is the smaller, everyday jobs — the ones that keep your car safe and reliable without the dealership markup.

Brakes & Rotors
Pads, rotors, calipers, and fluid. Squeaking, grinding, or a soft pedal — we'll get you stopping confidently again.
Oil & Fluid Changes
Full-service oil changes plus coolant, transmission, brake, and power-steering fluids — keeping everything topped off and clean.
Tires, Rotation & Alignment
Mount, balance, rotate, and align. We'll help you get even tread wear and a straight, smooth ride.
Batteries & Electrical
Battery testing and replacement, alternators, starters, lights, and the small electrical gremlins that stop a car from starting.
Diagnostics & Check-Engine
Check-engine light on? We'll scan the codes, pinpoint the real problem, and tell you straight what it actually needs.
Tune-Ups & Maintenance
Spark plugs, belts, hoses, filters, and scheduled service — the routine care that prevents the big, expensive repairs later.
…and just about anything else
Bigger jobs too — engines, transmissions, suspension, AC & heating. Not sure if we handle it? Just ask. If it drives, chances are we can help.
